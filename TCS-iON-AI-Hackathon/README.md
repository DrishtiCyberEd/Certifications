## 💡 About My Winning Solution – OT Alert Coach

OT Alert Coach is an AI-powered concept developed to help manufacturing and maintenance teams understand Operational Technology (OT) security alerts.

The solution takes simulated OT alerts and transforms technical information into easy-to-understand production-focused explanations.

It was designed to make industrial cybersecurity information more accessible to people working with manufacturing and automation systems.
